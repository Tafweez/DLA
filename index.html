Oologah Lake Dam Hydroelectric Project

FERC Project No. P-15353-002  |  Verdigris River, Rogers County, Oklahoma  |  Hybrid Renewables LLC

Hybrid Renewables LLC (HRLLC) is developing a 20-megawatt hydroelectric project at the existing U.S. Army Corps of Engineers Oologah Lake Dam under the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission's Traditional Licensing Process. This page provides the public documents in the licensing proceeding. The official record is on FERC eLibrary under docket P-15353.
